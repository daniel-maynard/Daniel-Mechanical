How it works

A clear path from idea
to prototype.

Small projects stay organized with clear requirements, agreed deliverables, and review points along the way.

01 / DISCOVER

Understand the need

Discuss the intended function, dimensions, interfaces, constraints, and desired outcome.

02 / DESIGN

Build the model

Create a parametric CAD model or assembly and share a review image before finalizing.

03 / PROTOTYPE

Make it tangible

Where appropriate, produce a 3D-printed prototype for basic fit and form review.

04 / DELIVER

Package the files

Provide the agreed STEP, STL, and native CAD deliverables with a brief handoff note.

About Vector Mechanical

Curiosity, precision, and practical design.

Vector Mechanical is an independent mechanical design studio focused on functional CAD and hands-on prototyping.

Designed around function.

The focus is on understanding how components fit, move, and interact — then translating those requirements into clear digital models and practical prototypes.

✓ Parametric modeling
✓ Mechanical assemblies
✓ Gears and transmissions
✓ Functional mechanisms
✓ Robotics components
✓ Electronics enclosures

Design and prototyping services are offered for suitable, non-safety-critical applications. Final designs should be independently reviewed and validated for their intended use.
